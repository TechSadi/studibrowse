How it works

From alone to focused in a minute.

No matching, no scheduling. Open a room or jump into one that's already going.

Open a room

Name it, set the timer and a few room goals. You're the host.

Share the link

Send it to classmates, or keep it public so anyone online can join. Up to 20 people.

Focus together

One shared timer for everyone. Chat in the breaks, share notes and links.

Built for real study sessions

Everything a good study group needs.

A shared Pomodoro timer

The host sets focus and break lengths and rounds. Everyone sees the same clock, live.

See who's studying

Live list of people in the room, what they're working on, and who's on a break.

Chat and share resources

Text chat, plus a shared shelf for PDFs and links, so nothing gets lost.

Room goals

Agree on what the room should finish tonight, and tick goals off together.

Follow your study people

Get notified when someone you follow opens a room, so you never miss a session.

Track your progress

Every round you finish is logged, with a weekly goal and a streak to keep.
